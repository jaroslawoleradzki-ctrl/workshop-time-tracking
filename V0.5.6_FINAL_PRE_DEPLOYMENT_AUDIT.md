## 4. Historical reconciliation implementation verification

### Original defect and affected workflow

Before v0.5.6, closure mode included only OPEN orders and CLOSED orders whose current `completionDate` was inside the report range. Thus a work-time entry recorded in August could remain in employee totals after its order was closed in September, while the order disappeared from closure order totals. The result was a false negative reconciliation difference (for example `-35`) and diagnostic “Zlecenie nieobjęte raportem zamknięcia.”

Affected paths are `getOrderReportRows()` (closure report and shared XLSX export) and `getReconciliationDiagnostics()` (called by `getClosureControlSummary()`), in `backend/src/routes/analytics.ts`.

### Actual solution at HEAD

For `closureReport=true`, `getOrderReportRows()` adds a third Prisma `OR` branch:

- status is `OPEN` or `CLOSED`;
- a related report exists with `deletedAt: null` and a date in the requested range.

`getReconciliationDiagnostics()` has the equivalent branch with the same inclusive date range. The selected/summed report relation is separately restricted to non-deleted reports in that same range. The predicate changes membership only; it does not introduce a second aggregation path.

Consequences verified from source and tests:

- A CLOSED order completed after the report period but with in-range time remains in reconciliation and in the closure order report.
- An OPEN/CLOSED order with only out-of-range or soft-deleted reports cannot qualify through the new branch.
- `SUSPENDED` and deleted orders remain excluded.
- CLOSED orders completed within the range retain their previous zero-hour inclusion behavior.
- Standard reports (`closureReport=false`), status filtering, and `onlyWithHours` remain on their prior path.
- `GET /api/analytics/export/by-order?closureReport=true` shares `getOrderReportRows()`, so the fixed membership and range-limited total flow into XLSX.

No code in this diff writes, deletes, or updates historical reports/orders; it consists of read/query qualification and test changes. Historical records are protected by `deletedAt: null` qualification/summing and by the absence of mutating operations.
