## v0.5.7 scope

Order progress is calculated cumulatively from the order's `orderDate` through the end of the selected reporting period. `actualHours` remains period-specific; `deviation` and percentage progress use cumulative active hours. Future-period and soft-deleted reports are excluded. The v0.5.6 historical reconciliation behavior for later-closed orders remains preserved. JSON, UI, XLSX, and CSV use consistent values.
